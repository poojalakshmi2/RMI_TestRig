# Buildlog
Contains the timeline of the project flow.

## 21 Aug - 23 Aug : Initial project idea
- Breaking down of the project into two parts: the turret and the hand held controller. This helped me to work on them indivitually.
- Decided the required components and determined the voltage levels.
### Issues:
- Initial turret design had signs of instability
- Hadn't thought of how exactly the shooting mechanism would be carried out.

## 24 Aug - 31 Aug : CAD Design
- One week was dedicated to solving the instability problem.
- Started designing each part of the turret and determining their appropriate dimensions.
### Not yet resolved :
- The main shooting portion still remains unresolved.

## 1 Sept - 3 Sept : Shooting mechanism work
- Firstly, analysed the orientation of the launcher with respect to the pan and tilt servo.
- Later figured out how exactly the barrel is going to be mounted with respect to the tilt servo.

## 4 Sept - 7 Sept : Figured out Shooting Mechanism
- On a design level, the shooter will contain a barrel and magazine. Small balls can be used to fire the target.
- Flywheel mechanism will be employed to give momentum to the balls.
### Works to be done:
- CAD the barrel, magazine and flywheel mechanism
- Overall CAD Assembly.
